About me

I’m Jin Sakai, a passionate video game designer dedicated to creating immersive experiences that blend art, storytelling, and technology. From an early age, I’ve been fascinated by how video games can emotionally connect with people, inspire them, and tell stories in a truly interactive way. Throughout my journey, I’ve developed strong technical skills in Blender, Unreal Engine, Unity, and Godot, complemented by experience in web design using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Astro. This combination allows me to approach projects from both an artistic and technical perspective — ensuring that visual design and functionality work hand in hand. My professional goal is to become a lead designer on a AAA title, contributing to a project that stands out not only for its artistic excellence but also for its emotional impact on players. I’m constantly looking to refine my skills, explore new tools, and collaborate with creative teams who share my passion for innovation and interactive art.

Designer on Ghost of Yotei

During my work on Ghost of Yotei, I served as a Game Designer focused on level design, combat balance, and player immersion. I collaborated closely with artists and programmers to build dynamic environments inspired by Japanese landscapes, ensuring that gameplay and narrative elements worked together seamlessly. This experience strengthened my ability to prototype gameplay mechanics, optimize player flow, and maintain a consistent artistic vision throughout the project. Working on Ghost of Yotei helped me refine my creative process and reinforced my passion for developing meaningful, visually stunning experiences.
